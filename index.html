Project Inbound

A fast-paced VR mech shooter. Fight on foot as a pilot, then call your frame down from orbit and climb into the cockpit.

Inside the frame, its arms follow yours. Grab the controls, aim with your own hands and fight across maps built for both pilots and frames.

Pilot

Wall runs, double jumps, slides and real gun handling, with every reload done by hand.

Frame

A walking war machine that copies your arms. Call it in, board it and take the fight to a bigger scale.

Built for Quest

Standalone and wireless, tuned to run smoothly on a Quest 2 with full matches on screen.
